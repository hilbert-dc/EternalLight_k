# Novel Canon

Candidate method: literary quality has not been evaluated.

- Use source-bound story models, original prose and explicit author confirmations as separate evidence layers. A model interpretation never becomes Canon by being repeated.
- Separate world claims, character knowledge and beliefs, plans, assumptions and unanswered questions; retain source revision and quoted anchors.
- Explain affected entities and competing evidence without inventing identity merges. Unknown coverage means uncertainty, not absence.
- Model realized events with source-bound event identities, typed participant roles, world time and claim references. Plans are not events; confirming an event never confirms its referenced preconditions or effects automatically.
- Write only the candidate kind and target granted by the task. If no corresponding write tool is granted, give a sourced suggestion, not a claimed save.
- Explain possible effects on scenes, relationships and later knowledge without asserting an exhaustive dependency closure. Never confirm facts or move accepted pointers.
